# Truvora user guide

Truvora helps you keep track of personal or business income, expenses, and
recurring subscriptions. This guide describes the features currently available
in the app.

## Get started

1. Sign in or create an account using the sign-in options shown by Truvora.
2. On your first visit, choose whether you will use Truvora for personal
   records, business records, or both.
3. If you choose business or both, enter a business name. You can also skip
   setup and go to the dashboard.
4. Add your first expense or subscription from the setup screen, or open the
   corresponding section later.

## Keep records separate

Use the ledger selector at the top of the app to switch between personal and
business records. Each ledger has its own expenses, income, and subscriptions;
check the selected ledger before adding or reviewing records. To add a business
later, open the selector and choose **Add business**.

## Dashboard

The dashboard summarizes spending for the current month, including expenses
and subscriptions. It also shows spending charts, category totals, and recent
transactions. These summaries reflect the currently selected ledger.

## Expenses and income

Open **Expenses** and select **Create**. Choose the **Expenses** or **Income**
tab, then enter a title, amount, date, and category. You can also add a note.
Amounts must be greater than zero. The currency is currently INR (Indian
Rupee).

Select **Add expense** or **Add income** to save. Existing records can be
updated with **Edit** or removed with **Delete**; deletion cannot be undone.
Some plans may limit custom categories and CSV exports.

## Subscriptions

Open **Subscriptions** and select **Create**. Enter the subscription name,
price, start date, billing cycle (monthly or yearly), and category. A note and
end date are optional. Leave the end date unset for a subscription that has no
planned end date.

Use **Edit** to change its details or mark it inactive. Inactive subscriptions
are excluded from active totals. Use **Delete** to remove a subscription and
its related ledger entries; this cannot be undone. The number of active
subscriptions may be limited by your plan.

## Transactions and exports

**Transactions** shows a paginated history of income, expenses, and
subscriptions in the selected ledger. Select **Sort Date** to switch the date
order. Where your plan allows it, use **Export** on the relevant page to
download a CSV file. Keep downloaded files somewhere secure.

## Plans and features

Open the account menu and select **Billing** to see your current plan and
available plans. Some features, such as custom categories, subscription
capacity, or exports, may depend on your plan. Paid checkout and plan upgrades
are not currently available in the app.

## Privacy and account deletion

See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) for details about data handling,
security, retention, and your privacy requests. Encryption applies to selected
fields; it does not cover every piece of account or financial data.

To delete your account and personal financial data, open **Settings** and
select **Delete my account**. Confirm only if you intend to proceed: deletion
cannot be undone. If cleanup is still processing, return to Settings and
select **Check deletion status**. Limited billing totals may be retained where
required, and backups or provider operational logs may persist temporarily;
see the Privacy Policy for details.

## Not available yet

- **Reports & Insights** is under development.
- Connecting a payment provider is not available.
- Paid checkout and plan upgrades are not available.

Truvora currently records information you enter; it does not connect to a bank
or payment provider to import transactions.
